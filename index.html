A few straight answers
Do you only manage Meta ads?

Meta ads are our focus: campaign setup, testing, optimisation and reporting. We also examine the store and measurement behind those campaigns so the ads have a better foundation.

Can you guarantee a ROAS?

Anyone who guarantees ROAS before seeing your margins is guessing with your money. We start with the business, the data and what can realistically be improved.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. Campaigns run in your account, and you pay Meta directly. Access and campaign history stay with your business.
