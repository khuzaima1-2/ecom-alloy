Close to the work.
Closer to your business.

Ecom Alloy is a founder-led marketing practice based in Dera Ghazi Khan, Pakistan. Founded by Mateen, our focus is Meta ads: researching, setting up, running and improving campaigns.

We start by asking how your business works. What do customers buy? What does it cost to fulfil an order? Where do people drop off? Those answers shape the work.

You'll speak directly with the person managing your campaigns. Clear conversations, clear decisions and a clear view of what happens next.
